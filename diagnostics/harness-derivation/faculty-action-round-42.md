## Capabilities it proposed

### "“Carry this out across my devices, and keep me informed of each handoff; if one device is unavailable, pause safely and tell me exactly what remains.”"
- **useful because:** Today the mind can decide a multi-surface plan, but action is fragmented: a Mac job, browser command, relay job, and pendant receipt can each look successful independently. This gives the owner one execution transaction with dependency-aware handoffs, explicit queued/running/blocked/verified states, and a truthful final receipt even after a dropped link or partial completion.
- **path:** faculty-judgement emits a signed dependency graph and irreversible-step checkpoints → faculty-action leases each step to mac-planner/mac-vision, browser-extension, or relay; records idempotency key and precondition snapshot → mac-planner/mac-terminal execute typed Mac work and return structured evidence; browser-extension executes private-tab work and returns tab/url/locator evidence → relay stays awake, persists the transaction and pushes concise progress to the pendant; pendant shows the current step, blocked reason, and requires local confirmation for physical/irreversible transitions → unified reconciles late receipts and presents one completion/partial/failure summary with undo or resume options
- **model tier:** Use deterministic state-machine and receipt verification for routing, leases, retries, and reconciliation; gpt-4.1-mini background for summarizing progress; reserve gpt-5.6-luna planner for ambiguous recovery only, and realtime solely for the live spoken update.
- **latency:** Acknowledge and display the lease within 300 ms; each handoff update within 1 s of receipt. Recovery/reconciliation may run in the background after the owner leaves.
- **cost:** Low for deterministic D1/state-machine work; roughly one background summarization call per completed transaction, with planner escalation only on ambiguity. Dominant cost is evidence/context summarization, not each action step.
- **security:** Private browser content and Mac evidence remain on their local surfaces unless the owner-approved transaction explicitly permits relay summarization. Never replay an irreversible step without an idempotency key and fresh precondition proof. Pause on permission loss, stale UI, timezone conflict, or receipt trust unknown; pendant confirmation is required before sending, purchasing, deleting, or physical actions.
- **missing:** A durable cross-surface transaction schema with dependency graph, leases, idempotency keys, precondition snapshots, and typed receipt states; A relay push channel and pendant progress UI for transaction updates and blocked reasons; A common evidence envelope (surface, timestamp, source locator, before/after hash, trust) accepted by Mac, browser, and relay; Resume/rollback policy and deterministic reconciliation for late or duplicate receipts; Accessibility and Screen Recording grants plus a live browser bridge for GUI/private-tab execution

### "“Make this change everywhere it needs to change, show me the conflicts and side effects first, then commit the whole change—or leave everything exactly as it was.”"
- **useful because:** The owner cannot currently make a coordinated change across Calendar, reminders, private web accounts, and local files without manually tracking partial updates. This provides a true cross-system change set: discover dependencies, simulate consequences, obtain one understandable approval, and either commit all safe steps or recover every already-applied reversible step.
- **path:** faculty-perception builds a dependency map from the owner's calendar, reminders, open authenticated tabs, and relevant Mac documents → faculty-judgement turns the requested change into a versioned change set, identifies conflicts, distinguishes reversible from irreversible effects, and presents a compact pendant approval summary → faculty-action asks browser-extension and Mac agents to stage changes in isolated drafts or temporary records, validates post-stage state, then commits in dependency order → relay persists the change-set, approval token, and compensating actions so the owner can leave during staging and resume later → pendant presents the conflict list and final diff audibly/through LEDs/buttons; one confirmation is bound to the exact change-set hash → unified reports committed, partially committed with compensations, or safely abandoned—never a vague success
- **model tier:** Deterministic diffing, dependency ordering, approval binding, and compensation rules first; gpt-4.1-mini for extracting and summarizing conflicts; gpt-5.6-luna only for ambiguous cross-application dependency reasoning; realtime only for the owner's live explanation.
- **latency:** Initial dependency scan within 5 seconds for local data and already-open tabs; staged diff within 30 seconds. Commit can continue in the background, with a pendant update at every system boundary.
- **cost:** One background extraction/summarization call per change set and planner escalation only when dependency semantics are unclear. Storage and API costs are modest; the dominant cost is authenticated-page and document inspection.
- **security:** Private account data must stay on the Mac/browser surfaces unless explicitly included in the change set. Approval tokens expire, are one-time, and bind to a plan hash plus precondition versions. No irreversible external send, purchase, deletion, or cancellation is included in an atomic commit unless separately confirmed. If any precondition changes after staging, commit pauses rather than overwriting.
- **missing:** A versioned cross-application change-set and compensating-action model, distinct from ordinary job receipts; Staging adapters for Calendar, Reminders, local documents, and authenticated browser forms; Conflict and side-effect extraction with before/after diffs understandable through the pendant; A commit coordinator that validates all preconditions immediately before each step and safely compensates reversible prior steps; A confirmation token bound cryptographically to the exact staged diff and its precondition snapshot


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `integration` — Introduce a cross-surface ActionTransaction protocol shared by faculty-judgement, faculty-action, Mac agent, browser bridge, relay, and pendant. Each transaction contains an immutable plan hash, step dependency graph, lease owner/expiry, idempotency key, precondition snapshot, and typed receipt (queued/running/blocked/completed/verified/unknown) with evidence and trust. Relay persists it; late or duplicate receipts reconcile deterministically; pendant receives progress and blocked reasons. Irreversible steps cannot transition to verified without fresh before/after proof and the required owner confirmation.
- **owner gets:** A single request that spans private browser tabs, Mac apps, and a long-running relay job will stop appearing mysteriously half-done. The owner gets one truthful spoken status, can leave and return, safely resume after a dropped connection, and know exactly which step happened and which did not.
- effort: Medium-high: protocol/types, D1 persistence, adapters in Mac/browser/relay, pendant progress states, test fixtures for duplicate/late/permission-loss receipts.  ·  risk: A schema or reconciliation bug could suppress a needed retry or falsely merge two actions. Mitigate with append-only events, plan-hash/idempotency validation, lease expiry to blocked (never automatic replay of irreversible steps), and migration behind read-only shadow logging first.
- cost: Small relay storage and bandwidth increase per step; deterministic processing is negligible. One concise background summarization call per completed multi-step transaction if a spoken brief is needed; no realtime call per receipt.  ·  latency: Immediate local queued state; relay push under 1 second normally. Verification waits for evidence rather than timing out into a success claim.
- security: Evidence is scoped to its surface and sensitivity; private page text stays local unless explicitly allowed. Leases expire, confirmation tokens are one-time and bound to plan hash/step, and untrusted receipts are visible as such.
- depends on: faculty-judgement preflight verdict contract; common evidence envelope and verify_action_proof tool; relay durable job/event persistence and push to pendant; Mac Accessibility/Screen Recording permissions; live authenticated browser extension bridge
