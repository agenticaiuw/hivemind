## Capabilities it proposed

### "When I say “I’m leaving” or “I’m on my way,” assemble a departure handoff: check my next calendar commitment, relevant logged-in travel or reservation tabs, unfinished Mac work, and anything I explicitly marked to bring up; give me one 20-second spoken checklist on the pendant and leave the full cited packet on my Mac. Never send or change anything."
- **useful because:** The moment of transition is where people forget the one detail that matters. This turns four disconnected surfaces into a dependable handoff, rather than another generic morning brief.
- **path:** pendant → relay → mac-planner → browser → dashboard
- **model tier:** background for gathering and ranking; realtime only for the short pendant response
- **latency:** 5–10 seconds for the packet; under 2 seconds to start the spoken checklist
- **cost:** About $0.01–$0.05 per handoff; most cost is authenticated-page extraction, not the spoken sentence
- **security:** Private calendar and logged-in reservation data stay on the Mac/browser; relay receives only the ranked checklist and citations. No booking, message, or file action without a separate request.
- **missing:** a transition trigger that can be spoken or generated by a pendant button; cross-surface packet schema with freshness and cited source fields; USB-worn audio delivery while LTE is unregistered

### "Before I commit to a consequential choice, let me ask “what if I don’t do this?” Have the system compare the relevant calendar commitments, open browser obligations, reminders, and active projects, then give me a compact consequence map: what breaks, by when, who is affected, and what the least-cost alternative is. Do not take action."
- **useful because:** Most assistants optimize the requested action; they do not help a person see the cost of inaction. This is a decision aid grounded in the owner’s real commitments, not generic advice.
- **path:** pendant → relay → mac-planner → browser → dashboard
- **model tier:** background reasoning model; realtime only when the owner asks the question aloud
- **latency:** 10–20 seconds for a useful map; a spoken top-line in under 3 seconds if sources are already cached
- **cost:** About $0.03–$0.15 per map, dominated by synthesis over private state; cache unchanged source facts
- **security:** Treat inferred consequences as hypotheses, label confidence and source age, and keep private page contents local. Never contact a person or alter a deadline automatically.
- **missing:** a typed consequence/uncertainty graph with explicit counterfactual links; a way to distinguish hard commitments from soft intentions; a citation-rich spoken response format

### "Let me say a standing boundary such as “never send work mail after 8 pm” or “ask before opening financial pages,” then have the system turn it into a plain-language policy, test it against Mac, browser, relay, and pendant examples, and enforce it everywhere. If a case is ambiguous, ask me one question instead of guessing."
- **useful because:** The owner should not have to renegotiate safety and privacy one action at a time. A single policy expressed once should follow them across the wearable, private browser, acting Mac, and always-awake relay.
- **path:** pendant → relay → mac-planner → browser → dashboard
- **model tier:** background model compiles and tests policies; realtime handles only an in-the-moment ambiguity
- **latency:** Policy creation in 10–30 seconds; enforcement decision under 300 ms from a proposed action
- **cost:** Roughly $0.02–$0.10 to compile/test a policy; enforcement should be local rule evaluation with negligible per-action cost
- **security:** Policies themselves can reveal sensitive habits, so store encrypted and expose only the minimum rule to each surface. Fail closed for destructive, financial, or communication actions; provide an explicit emergency override with confirmation.
- **missing:** one shared policy language and evaluator used by all surfaces; policy simulation against recorded action intents without executing them; a durable versioned policy receipt explaining why an action was blocked

### "During a meeting, let me say “quiet coach.” The pendant should privately listen for questions or decisions, consult the relevant calendar event, open Mac documents, and authenticated browser tabs, then whisper a short suggested answer or say “I’m not sure.” After the meeting, leave a cited list of questions I could not answer. Never speak aloud to the room or send anything."
- **useful because:** This would give the owner an always-available private second brain in the moment decisions are made, without forcing them to interrupt the meeting to search or type.
- **path:** pendant → relay-realtime → mac-planner → browser-extension → mac-vision
- **model tier:** realtime for speech turn detection and concise suggestions; background for document retrieval and post-meeting unanswered-question review
- **latency:** 1–2 seconds from a detected question to a whispered suggestion; under 30 seconds for the post-meeting list
- **cost:** Approximately $0.10–$0.50 per meeting depending on audio duration and retrieved context; retrieval and realtime transcription dominate
- **security:** Meeting audio and private documents are highly sensitive. Require an explicit spoken activation per meeting, visible recording state, local redaction of unrelated speech, short retention, and a hard mute button on the pendant. Suggestions must show confidence and source links when reviewed.
- **missing:** local or trusted meeting speech segmentation and diarization; a private low-latency audio return channel to the pendant; event-scoped retrieval across Mac documents and authenticated tabs; a meeting lifecycle that expires automatically

### "When I say “teach me this,” turn a topic from my current browser page, Mac document, or voice question into a private learning loop: explain it briefly, ask me one retrieval question through the pendant, adapt the next question to my answer, and bring it back days later until I can use it. Keep a small progress record instead of replaying the whole lesson."
- **useful because:** The system would convert the information it already finds for the owner into durable understanding, rather than producing one more briefing that is forgotten.
- **path:** pendant → relay → browser-extension → mac-planner → dashboard
- **model tier:** background model for lesson and spaced-review generation; realtime only for the short spoken quiz and answer
- **latency:** Initial lesson in 10 seconds; each spoken question/feedback turn under 2 seconds; scheduled reviews can run asynchronously
- **cost:** About $0.03–$0.15 per lesson and less than $0.02 per review; synthesis is the main cost
- **security:** Do not retain source pages or sensitive work content beyond the minimum extracted concepts. Let the owner mark topics confidential, and make review audio opt-in during quiet hours.
- **missing:** a durable concept/proficiency record separate from general memory; a scheduler that chooses review timing from demonstrated recall; an audio queue with answer capture and cancellation

### "Let me ask “what am I avoiding?” The system should inspect unfinished projects, repeatedly postponed reminders, stale browser tabs, unsent drafts, and calendar commitments, then return at most three avoidance hypotheses with evidence and one tiny reversible next step for each. It must distinguish lack of time, uncertainty, missing information, and unwillingness, and let me reject a hypothesis permanently."
- **useful because:** A personal assistant should notice recurring friction that ordinary to-do lists hide, while preserving the owner’s agency and avoiding the judgmental certainty of a generic productivity coach.
- **path:** pendant → relay → mac-planner → browser-extension → dashboard
- **model tier:** background reasoning model; realtime only to deliver the short result and capture rejection/correction
- **latency:** 20–60 seconds for a cross-surface scan; under 3 seconds to speak a cached result
- **cost:** Approximately $0.05–$0.20 per scan, dominated by private-state synthesis; run only when explicitly requested
- **security:** This infers sensitive mental and work patterns. Keep evidence local, explain every hypothesis, never expose it to another person or scheduled briefing, and support immediate deletion of the inference and its sources.
- **missing:** a private, user-correctable pattern store for rejected and confirmed hypotheses; cross-surface temporal analysis of postponements rather than simple task listing; a non-actioning micro-step generator with explicit reversibility
