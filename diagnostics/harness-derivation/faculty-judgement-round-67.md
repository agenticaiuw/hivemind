## Capabilities it proposed

### "“Stop everything.” (or long-press the pendant button) should immediately silence the pendant, freeze new automation, cancel queued Mac/browser work, and give me a private receipt of what was stopped; “resume” should show the exact next unverified steps before anything continues."
- **useful because:** A person wearing an agent needs a physical, reliable boundary when a meeting starts, a private conversation begins, or an automation looks wrong. This is stronger than a conversational promise: it works during a dropped link, prevents queued actions from racing ahead, and makes trust recoverable rather than requiring the owner to remember which surface is active.
- **path:** pendant → relay-realtime → mac-planner → browser-extension → mac-terminal → unified → faculty-perception → faculty-action
- **model tier:** No expensive model for the stop path; pendant firmware and relay perform deterministic cancellation. Use the cheap background tier only to summarize the stopped jobs. Use realtime only to speak the one-sentence receipt or ask for resume confirmation.
- **latency:** Local mute/LED state under 100 ms; relay cancellation and browser/Mac freeze target under 1 s when linked. Offline, the pendant remains muted and records a bounded stop marker until reconnect. Resume requires an explicit owner action and a short evidence/readback pass, not model deliberation.
- **cost:** Near-zero API cost for stop/cancel; one short receipt generation is typically <$0.01 and can be omitted. Engineering cost is a firmware event plus authenticated relay fan-out and adapters for existing job/browser/pipeline stores.
- **security:** The stop command must be accepted locally without network and authenticated on reconnect; it must not transmit microphone audio while latched. Cancellation should be idempotent and append-only, and receipts should contain job IDs/status—not page contents or secrets. “Resume” must never replay an irreversible step; it must require fresh preconditions and confirmation for send/delete/purchase.
- **missing:** A pendant-local privacy latch with long-press debounce and persistent stop marker (single button/LED is available, but this behavior is not established).; A relay-wide freeze token checked by every new job/action and fan-out cancellation to Mac jobs, browser commands, and audio pipelines.; A durable stop/resume receipt schema that integrates faculty-action's requested/attempted/witnessed/confirmed checkpoint protocol and exposes the next unverified step.; A small owner-facing resume review UI/audio flow; macOS Accessibility remains unnecessary because cancellation can use the existing authenticated routes.

### "“Private mode” should follow me across the pendant, Mac, and browser: when I tap the pendant or enter a sensitive calendar context, the system should stop speaking private content aloud, prevent private page text from entering model context, pause background jobs that could expose it, and show me a small local indication of the active boundary. When I leave, it should not silently resume disclosure; it should give me a concise review of what was withheld and let me restore normal mode."
- **useful because:** Today privacy is a collection of per-surface settings, so the owner must remember whether the pendant is audible, which browser tabs are logged in, and which background jobs are running. A single privacy boundary that travels with the person would let them use an always-listening assistant around other people without gambling that one surface forgot the rule. The important behavior is omission and fail-closed handling, not merely deleting logs afterward.
- **path:** pendant → relay-realtime → mac-planner → browser-extension → mac-terminal → unified → faculty-perception → faculty-judgement → faculty-action
- **model tier:** Deterministic policy evaluation and redaction should handle the hot path; use a cheaper background model to summarize withheld items. Realtime should speak only a generic confirmation such as “Private mode is on,” never the protected content. No model call is needed to enforce the boundary.
- **latency:** Pendant indication and local microphone/playback policy under 100 ms. Relay and Mac/browser policy propagation under 1 s when connected. On link loss, the pendant remains private and the Mac/browser agents fail closed until an explicit owner-controlled rejoin.
- **cost:** Negligible inference cost on enforcement; occasional background summaries are <$0.01 per mode transition. Main cost is a shared policy protocol, redaction boundary in context assembly, and integration tests across reconnects and tab/job races.
- **security:** A sensitive mode must not be inferred solely from an untrusted page title or calendar text; explicit pendant action always wins. Store only mode, scope, timestamps, and reason—not protected content. Existing bearer-authenticated sessions need per-mode capability tokens, and every withheld/allowed decision needs an auditable reason. Leaving private mode must require an explicit tap or spoken confirmation and must not replay queued speech or actions automatically.
- **missing:** A signed, device-originated privacy-mode token with scope (audio, browser, Mac jobs, context) and monotonic expiry/revocation.; A policy enforcement point before transcription, before model-context projection, before TTS, and before browser/Mac job execution; current surfaces do not share one fail-closed token.; A redacted withheld-items ledger that can report counts and sources without retaining their contents.; A pendant-local privacy state machine and distinct LED/vibration/audio indication; it must survive a dropped link and reboot safely.; A reconnect protocol that revalidates every paused job and speech item instead of replaying stale private material.


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `firmware` — Add a pendant-local SafetyLatch state machine: a debounced 1.5–2 s long press enters STOPPED, immediately gates microphone capture and playback, flashes the single LED in a distinct pattern, persists only {epoch, latch counter, firmware version, reason=manual} in a tiny flash record, and emits one signed stop marker on the next link. A second deliberate long press never resumes automatically; it only requests a review state. Relay middleware should treat the marker as a kill token, atomically reject new work, cancel existing Mac/browser jobs, drain audio, and publish a receipt. Resume is a separate authenticated action after faculty-action verifies each checkpoint and presents the next unverified step.
- **owner gets:** The owner can create a private, quiet, non-negotiable boundary without finding the phone, speaking aloud, or trusting a network. If the link drops, the boundary still holds; when it returns, the system can explain exactly what did and did not happen instead of silently resuming.
- effort: Medium: firmware event/state + flash record, relay latch middleware, adapters around existing job/browser/pipeline routes, and tests for link loss, duplicate markers, and interrupted irreversible actions.  ·  risk: A false long press could interrupt a briefing, and a stale latch could make the agent appear dead. Use a distinct hold duration, LED feedback, explicit resume, watchdog expiry only for diagnostics (never auto-resume), and a local audible/LED acknowledgment. Recovery is a deliberate resume review, not replay.
- cost: Negligible runtime/API cost. A few hundred bytes of flash and under 1 KB RAM; no new hardware is required beyond the existing single button/LED. A production device should add a recessed or capacitive privacy button (~$0.20–$2, negligible power) so a pocket cannot trigger it.  ·  latency: Local mute is sub-100 ms after debounce; network cancellation is best-effort under 1 s. Resume adds a human review step, intentionally increasing latency only for safety-sensitive continuation.
- security: Improves privacy by stopping mic/playback locally. Signed monotonic markers prevent forged or replayed stop events; latch state must be fail-closed on reboot until explicit resume. Receipts must redact page content and secrets.
- depends on: faculty-action's checkpoint/resume protocol with explicit next-unverified-step semantics; A relay-wide freeze token and cancellation fan-out across Mac jobs, browser commands, and audio pipelines; A typed receipt/claim store for stop, cancellation, and resume evidence
