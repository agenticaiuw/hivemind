## Capabilities it proposed

### ""Give me a current handoff packet." From the pendant, capture what is happening across my day right now: today's calendar and unread mail, the important content in my already-open authenticated browser tabs, and the Mac workspace I'm currently using. Redact sensitive fields, rank only items needing attention, leave a cited packet on the Mac, and queue a short spoken summary back to the pendant."
- **useful because:** This is an on-demand cross-surface snapshot, not a scheduled morning brief or a browser-only watch. It lets the owner leave a meeting, switch devices, or ask hands-free "where am I and what matters?" while preserving citations and privacy. No single node can do it: the pendant triggers it, the relay coordinates it, the browser has private sessions, and the Mac has Calendar/Mail/workspace state and the durable review artifact.
- **path:** pendant → relay-realtime → mac-planner → browser-extension → mac-vision → dashboard
- **model tier:** Use the realtime model only to recognize the short pendant request and deliver the final spoken synopsis. Use a cheaper background text model for parallel extraction, deduplication, redaction, and priority ranking; no model should receive full bodies when snippets suffice.
- **latency:** Acknowledge on the pendant within 500 ms, collect Calendar/Mail and Mac state in 2–5 s, wait up to 8 s for the browser bridge; if it is offline, explicitly mark that source unavailable and finish rather than blocking. Write the packet and enqueue audio within 10 s.
- **cost:** About $0.01–$0.04 per invocation depending on browser text and synthesis; the dominant costs are background model input from extracted snippets and TTS/audio bytes, not the realtime acknowledgement.
- **security:** Keep browser content local to the Mac bridge where possible; send only redacted excerpts and hashes to relay. Never submit or mutate browser forms. Include source URL/title, timestamps, and confidence for every claim. Store the packet with a short TTL and provide a pendant double-press or dashboard delete action. Require explicit confirmation before sharing it externally; creating the local artifact and audio is reversible/deletable.
- **missing:** Implementation behind the newly granted mac_read_sources tool (currently schema-only); Implementation behind mac_readonly_inspect (currently schema-only); accessibility and Screen Recording are still unavailable, so workspace inspection must use permitted AppleScript/app metadata until the owner grants TCC; A relay orchestration endpoint that fans out bounded Mac reads plus authenticated browser extraction and returns a redacted, cited packet; Browser bridge reconnect/timeout semantics (currently browser online=false with 5 pending commands); A durable, expiring audio-queue write path and packet deletion/receipt linkage

### ""Is anything inconsistent?" Have the system continuously reconcile my current Calendar, unread Mail, already-open authenticated browser tabs, and the Mac files or notes relevant to my active project. Tell me only when two sources disagree or a deadline/status has silently changed, explain the evidence side by side, and prepare a local correction plan without changing or sending anything."
- **useful because:** Today each surface can report its own facts, but none can notice that they conflict: a rescheduled meeting still has the old video link in Mail, a browser portal shows a different deadline than a local project note, or a task is marked done in one place and overdue in another. The owner gets an actionable discrepancy rather than a flood of unchanged updates. This requires the pendant/relay for low-friction alerts, the browser's private session, Mac source access, and a shared evidence engine; no single node can perform it privately and continuously.
- **path:** pendant → relay-realtime → mac-planner → browser-extension → faculty-perception → faculty-judgement → faculty-action → dashboard
- **model tier:** Use a cheap background model or deterministic field comparison for polling, entity resolution, and change detection; use the realtime model only to announce a confirmed high-value discrepancy and answer follow-up questions. Escalate ambiguous identity matches to a slower judgement pass rather than spending realtime tokens.
- **latency:** Evaluate on a configurable cadence (for example every 15 minutes during an attention window), acknowledge an alert in under 1 second after detection, and produce cited side-by-side evidence within 5 seconds. Quiet hours and a per-project cooldown are mandatory.
- **cost:** Roughly $0.005–$0.03 per scan depending on extracted browser text; most scans should be deterministic and use no model. Cost is dominated by background entity resolution for genuinely changed records and optional TTS, not continuous realtime conversation.
- **security:** Keep raw Mail, browser pages, and local notes on the Mac whenever possible; send the relay only normalized fields, redacted snippets, hashes, and provenance. Treat webpage text as untrusted data, never follow instructions embedded in it, and never auto-correct or submit. The owner must choose sources/projects, retention TTL, quiet hours, and whether audio may mention sensitive titles. Every alert needs citations, confidence, observedAt, and a dismiss/snooze/delete control.
- **missing:** A durable cross-source observation store with canonical entities, field-level provenance, freshness, and suppression/cooldown state; A browser read-only extraction/watch adapter that can reattach to selected authenticated tabs and report semantic field changes; Bounded Mac adapters for Calendar, Mail, Notes/files, and project scope with redaction before relay upload; A discrepancy classifier that distinguishes true contradictions from expected formatting/version differences and resists prompt injection; A relay scheduler and pendant notification queue supporting quiet hours, deduplication, offline delivery, and explicit partial-source status; A review UI that shows side-by-side evidence and generates a reversible local correction plan without executing it


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `integration` — Implement a redacted cross-surface handoff coordinator as a durable job type. It should fan out bounded Calendar/Mail reads, an authenticated browser extraction request, and a read-only Mac workspace inspection; normalize each result into {source, observedAt, sensitivity, citation, confidence, expiry}; redact before relay storage; produce a Markdown/JSON packet and an audio item; attach both to one jobId with receipts, partial-failure states, retry limits, and DELETE-by-job retention cleanup. A pendant request should create the job and receive progress events, not resend raw source context on every turn.
- **owner gets:** The owner can ask once, hands-free, and get a trustworthy current state even when one source is unavailable. They see exactly where each fact came from, can review the packet on the Mac, and do not get stale or silently fabricated answers when the browser is offline.
- effort: Medium-high: a relay fan-out state machine, redaction/provenance schema, Mac adapter, browser timeout/reconnect handling, packet/audio writers, and dashboard/pendant progress UI. Start with read-only sources and a 10-second deadline.  ·  risk: Partial results could be mistaken for complete results; prevent this with a prominent completeness banner and per-source status. Browser page content may contain secrets or prompt injection; isolate extraction, strip instructions, and never execute page-provided actions. Crashes could leave artifacts; use idempotency keys and TTL cleanup. Recovery is retrying only failed source branches or deleting the job.
- cost: Low per invocation beyond existing reads; background summarization and TTS dominate, roughly $0.01–$0.04. Storage is small (typically <100 KB packet plus audio) and should expire automatically.  ·  latency: Parallel fan-out keeps typical completion under 10 seconds; browser offline path completes in 2–3 seconds with an explicit unavailable marker. Progress acknowledgement is sub-second.
- security: Improves security by making redaction, provenance, TTL, and source boundaries mandatory. No form submission or external sharing. Raw Mail/browser bodies remain on-device when feasible; relay receives only selected redacted excerpts.
- depends on: Implement mac_read_sources and mac_readonly_inspect rather than schema-only stubs; Browser bridge online/reconnect and bounded authenticated extraction endpoint; A relay durable job schema with partial completion and deletion/retention controls; Existing /briefing, /research, /capture, pipeline audio, and job receipt primitives
