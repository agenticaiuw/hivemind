## Capabilities it proposed

### "“Only interrupt me when it is genuinely worth breaking my concentration—and if I miss it, keep escalating until the right moment.”"
- **useful because:** Today notifications are sender-centric and every surface competes for attention. This would make the hive an attention steward: infer whether the owner is driving, speaking, in a meeting, deep in a Mac task, or idle; defer low-value items; bundle related events; and escalate a truly time-sensitive item through the pendant only when the interruption cost is justified. It is not a notification filter: it closes the loop across private browser evidence, Mac activity, relay schedules, and the wearable's real-time attention state.
- **path:** browser-extension: report narrowly scoped, authenticated event candidates (new appointment change, expiring form, urgent account event) with source evidence, never raw page contents → mac-planner/mac-vision: report active app/task, meeting state, unsaved work, call/video state, and whether an interruption can safely be rendered; optionally create a reversible deferred reminder → relay-realtime: maintain the durable urgency record, deduplicate related events, apply quiet hours/escalation timers, and send a compact spoken interruption or queue item → pendant: provide local interaction signals (currently speaking/listening, explicit do-not-disturb, acknowledge/snooze/escalate) and deliver the final low-latency prompt → unified/faculty-judgement: rank urgency × deadline × consequence against estimated interruption cost, explain the reason, and choose defer/bundle/interrupt/escalate
- **model tier:** Cheap background model classifies and deduplicates event candidates; scheduled relay jobs use a small model to re-rank as deadlines approach. Realtime is used only for the final spoken interruption and the owner's short response. High-risk decisions (financial, medical, sending/submitting) remain review-only and never auto-escalate into action.
- **latency:** Candidate ingestion under 2 seconds; ordinary events may wait up to the next natural break. A hard-deadline event should reach the pendant within 5 seconds, with configurable retry/escalation windows (for example 2, 10, and 30 minutes). Acknowledgement/snooze should be immediate locally even if the relay is temporarily disconnected.
- **cost:** Roughly <$0.01 per event batch using a small background model; realtime token cost only for an actual interruption (typically <$0.01–$0.03). Dominant cost is authenticated page polling/event extraction and long-lived relay timers, not judgement.
- **security:** Event payloads can reveal private mail, work, health, and location context. Keep raw page text on the Mac/browser boundary; send typed event, deadline, sensitivity, and evidence hash to relay. Encrypt durable records, enforce per-source scopes and TTLs, and show “why now / source / consequence” before any escalation. Never infer emergency status from weak signals or contact anyone automatically; owner-configured trusted channels and explicit confirmation are required.
- **missing:** A typed attention-event schema with sensitivity, deadline, consequence, source evidence, and expiry; A durable urgency/escalation state machine with deduplication, quiet hours, retry, snooze, and acknowledgement receipts; Mac focus/meeting/unsaved-work signals exposed as privacy-preserving typed state; Pendant-local DND, acknowledge, snooze, and offline queue primitives; A policy editor and audit view showing every suppressed, bundled, escalated, and expired event

### "“When my calendar, email, and web accounts disagree, don't guess—tell me what conflicts, ask the one question that resolves it, and make the correction everywhere it belongs.”"
- **useful because:** A personal assistant that silently chooses the freshest-looking source can cause missed travel, duplicate commitments, or wrong submissions. This is a cross-surface truth-repair workflow: identify a concrete contradiction, preserve both claims and provenance, ask one minimal clarification through the pendant, then update only owner-approved records and leave an audit trail. It turns uncertainty into a short interaction rather than a confident mistake.
- **path:** browser-extension: inspect only explicitly bound authenticated tabs and return conflicting fields with URLs, timestamps, and source snippets/hashes; prepare but do not submit edits → mac-planner/mac-terminal: read local calendar, notes, mail metadata, and task records; compute which records are writable and make reversible updates after approval → relay-realtime: persist a contradiction case, correlate evidence across reconnects, manage expiry, and route the question/answer to whichever surface owns the record → pendant: ask a single concise disambiguation question, offer two or three labeled choices, and capture an answer offline if the link drops → faculty-perception: establish whether records truly refer to the same event/person and score freshness/reliability → faculty-judgement: select the minimum question and decide whether correction is safe, draft-only, or requires explicit confirmation → faculty-action: apply approved updates with idempotency, before/after receipts, and undo
- **model tier:** Small background model for entity matching and contradiction clustering; judgement model only for ambiguous or consequential conflicts. Realtime is limited to asking the one question and confirming the proposed repair.
- **latency:** Detect during scheduled checks or on request; present a conflict in under 10 seconds. The owner's answer should be acknowledged locally within 300 ms, with updates and receipts completing asynchronously within a minute.
- **cost:** About $0.01–$0.04 per conflict, dominated by authenticated reads and any Mac/browser action; most routine matching should use a cheap model. Realtime spend is one short turn, not a full conversation.
- **security:** Conflicts may expose sensitive account contents. Keep source excerpts local where possible and transmit typed claims plus minimal redacted evidence. Never overwrite a source solely because it is newer; require field-level authorization, preserve old values, and require confirmation for financial, legal, medical, or externally visible changes.
- **missing:** A durable contradiction-case schema linking claims, entities, provenance, freshness, sensitivity, and resolution status; A field-level source-of-truth and write-permission policy rather than global account trust; A pendant choice/answer capture protocol that survives disconnection; Cross-surface transactional updates with receipts and rollback across Mac and browser; A conflict inbox so unresolved contradictions do not disappear into ordinary reminders

### "“Use my private accounts to get things done, but keep my passwords and unrelated personal data out of the AI—and show me exactly what each part was allowed to see.”"
- **useful because:** The owner cannot safely delegate across browser, Mac, relay, and pendant today without either exposing broad account contents to a model/server or refusing useful automation. This would provide least-privilege, purpose-bound delegation: the browser and Mac hold secrets and raw pages locally, while reasoning receives only redacted claims and evidence needed for the current task. It makes private automation trustworthy enough for daily use rather than an all-or-nothing choice.
- **path:** browser-extension: execute within the owner's authenticated session, enforce per-task origin/field scopes, redact secrets and unrelated DOM, and return typed results plus evidence hashes → mac-planner/mac-terminal: broker local files and account metadata through explicit capability grants, with path/field allowlists and a local audit log → relay-realtime: hold only short-lived task capabilities, redacted claims, consent state, and receipts; never receive reusable credentials or unrestricted page dumps → pendant: present a compact consent card/voice summary of what will be accessed, why, for how long, and where; allow revoke/pause even while offline → faculty-judgement: choose the minimum scopes needed, detect scope creep, and stop when the requested operation exceeds the grant → faculty-action: perform only operations authorized by the capability token and emit before/after evidence without retaining sensitive values
- **model tier:** Small background model compiles requested goals into scope manifests and detects unnecessary fields; realtime is used only for a short owner consent or revocation exchange. No expensive model should ever receive raw credentials.
- **latency:** Scope preview in under 2 seconds; local token issuance and revocation under 300 ms; ordinary browser/Mac actions may remain asynchronous. Revocation must take effect before the next external mutation, even if relay connectivity is lost.
- **cost:** Approximately $0.005–$0.02 per delegated task for policy compilation and receipt summarization; cryptographic checks and local redaction dominate engineering, not API usage.
- **security:** A compromised extension, Mac agent, relay, or model could otherwise become a universal account key. Use hardware-bound, short-lived, audience- and purpose-bound tokens; local redaction; nonce/idempotency checks; immutable access receipts; no secret persistence; and explicit confirmation for external sends, purchases, deletions, or permission changes. The owner must be able to revoke all outstanding grants from the pendant.
- **missing:** A cross-surface capability-token protocol with audience, purpose, fields, expiry, and mutation limits; Local browser and Mac redaction/secret-handling enforcement before any data reaches relay or model; A pendant consent and emergency revoke path that works without a live link; A tamper-evident access and mutation receipt view; Policy tests proving that a task cannot expand its scope by prompting the model

## Its own summary

Added two capabilities beyond the backlog: (1) an attention steward that fuses pendant attention state, Mac focus/meeting state, authenticated browser event candidates, and relay deadlines to defer, bundle, interrupt, and escalate only when worthwhile; (2) a contradiction-repair workflow that preserves conflicting calendar/mail/browser claims, asks one minimal pendant question, then makes field-level approved corrections with receipts and undo. I also told mac-vision there is no immediate owner-specific UI task.

**Biggest unknown:** The foundational cross-surface primitives are still not exposed in this run: typed attention events and privacy-preserving Mac focus signals; durable escalation/acknowledgement state; contradiction cases with source-of-truth/write policy; and pendant-local offline acknowledge/snooze capture. Without those, the proposals remain specifications rather than executable owner experiences.
