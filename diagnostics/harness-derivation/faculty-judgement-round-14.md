## Capabilities it proposed

### "When I make a consequential decision out loud, capture what I decided, the evidence and assumptions behind it, and—if I name a date—check back later and tell me whether reality matched the bet."
- **useful because:** People remember the conclusion but lose the reasoning, making repeated decisions and hindsight painful. This creates a private, searchable decision trail and a low-noise learning loop without requiring the owner to maintain another app. It is meaningfully more than a note: the pendant catches the moment, the Mac/browser assemble evidence, and the relay brings back the outcome at the right time.
- **path:** pendant: detect an explicit decision phrase or a button-marked moment, ask one brief confirmation before recording sensitive content, and provide a spoken/haptic receipt → relay: transcribe/classify the decision, store an encrypted decision packet and schedule a future check-in; remain available while the Mac is asleep → mac-planner: turn the packet into a structured Markdown record in ~/AI-Pendant-Workspace with decision, alternatives, assumptions, confidence, owner, and review date → browser-extension: gather cited facts from the currently relevant authenticated tabs, but only from tabs the owner has associated with the decision → mac-terminal: at review time, compare the recorded success criteria against current local files or explicitly permitted browser evidence and prepare a concise delta → unified: reconcile the evidence and ask whether to revise the decision record; never silently infer that an outcome is success or failure
- **model tier:** Use gpt-realtime-2.1 only for the pendant's live capture and confirmation; use gpt-5.6-luna for extraction, evidence reconciliation, and review; use a cheaper scheduled/background model for polling and drafting the review packet.
- **latency:** Receipt under 2 seconds after confirmation. Evidence assembly can finish in the background within 2 minutes. Review notifications should be quiet and arrive only at the named date or a meaningful evidence change.
- **cost:** Roughly $0.01–$0.06 per decision capture/review, dominated by realtime audio and authenticated-page extraction; scheduled unchanged checks should be pennies or less using hashes and a cheap model.
- **security:** Decision records may expose health, finances, or work strategy. Store locally by default, encrypt relay payloads, attach sensitivity and retention, and require confirmation before capturing an ambiguous or sensitive decision. Browser evidence must be allowlisted and cited; never send mail, submit forms, or change source data. The owner can delete a record and cancel its review.
- **missing:** A decision-record schema distinct from reminders/commitments, with assumptions, alternatives, evidence citations, success criteria, and review state; An event-to-file persistence and encrypted relay queue shared by pendant, Mac, and scheduled jobs; A user-controlled browser evidence binding (which tabs/sites may be consulted) and provenance-preserving extraction; A quiet review scheduler that can compare criteria without turning every watch into a notification; A compact pendant confirmation interaction and a private audio/haptic receipt

### "Keep this private: do the work, but don't say the answer out loud—show it on my Mac and give me only a discreet pendant signal when it's ready."
- **useful because:** A wearable assistant is otherwise unusable in meetings, transit, or around family. The owner can delegate sensitive or lengthy work without broadcasting either the request's result or its contents. The Mac/browser can present detail while the pendant remains a safe, minimal control surface.
- **path:** pendant: a long-press or spoken phrase enables private-output mode, acknowledges with a distinct haptic pattern, and speaks only a generic completion cue → relay: carries the privacy/output policy with every job, redacts result text from realtime playback, and queues a minimal notification if the Mac is temporarily unavailable → mac-planner: renders the result in a lock-screen-safe or unlocked workspace pane according to the owner's setting, with an explicit expiry → browser-extension: performs authenticated research or prepares drafts while keeping page text out of spoken responses → faculty-judgement: selects whether the result can be summarized publicly, privately displayed, or withheld until the owner unlocks the Mac → faculty-action: enforces the output policy at the final delivery boundary, not merely in the model prompt
- **model tier:** gpt-realtime-2.1 for the short live mode toggle only; gpt-5.6-luna for planning and synthesis; background work on a cheaper model. No realtime call is needed just to signal completion.
- **latency:** Mode acknowledgement under 300 ms; discreet completion signal under 1 second after the result is committed. Detailed Mac rendering can take normal task latency.
- **cost:** Negligible incremental API cost—mostly one short realtime turn per mode change. Hardware/software work dominates; polling should use push events rather than repeated model calls.
- **security:** A generic signal can still reveal that a private task exists, so offer neutral vibration and configurable quiet hours. Never put sensitive text in desktop notifications, logs, URLs, or TTS buffers. Clear private mode after a timeout or explicit toggle, and require confirmation before switching back to spoken output for a sensitive result.
- **missing:** A policy-carrying job envelope whose output channel and sensitivity survive relay, Mac, and browser handoffs; A pendant-local private-mode toggle and distinct haptic acknowledgement; A Mac presentation surface with lock-screen-safe redaction and automatic expiry; A final output firewall that blocks accidental TTS/log/notification leakage; Push completion events and a recovery path when the Mac is offline

### "Before I commit to this, show me the likely consequences of each option using my actual calendar, messages, files, and logged-in services—and tell me what assumption would change your recommendation."
- **useful because:** The owner can get generic advice today, but not a grounded, private decision rehearsal spanning the surfaces that contain their real constraints. This would turn scattered facts into an explicit comparison without taking the action or pretending uncertain predictions are facts.
- **path:** pendant: capture the decision question and constraints hands-free, then read only the recommendation and the key uncertainty aloud → relay: maintain a short-lived encrypted scenario packet and coordinate the work while the Mac or browser is busy → mac-planner: decompose options, identify required evidence, and generate a comparison with confidence and missing-data labels → browser-extension: inspect only owner-approved authenticated tabs for prices, policies, availability, or account-specific constraints, preserving citations → mac-terminal: inspect local calendars, files, and project state when permitted, calculate dates/costs, and run deterministic what-if checks → faculty-perception: distinguish observed facts from forecasts and detect contradictions across sources → faculty-judgement: rank options against the owner's stated priorities, expose tradeoffs, and ask for clarification when the result is sensitive → faculty-action: make no external change; save a reviewable scenario packet and optionally set a user-approved expiry
- **model tier:** Use gpt-realtime-2.1 only for the live question and concise handoff. Use gpt-5.6-luna for decomposition and reconciliation, and a cheaper background model for deterministic comparisons and evidence collection.
- **latency:** A first useful comparison in 10–20 seconds for local evidence; authenticated browsing may take up to 2 minutes in the background. The pendant should immediately acknowledge scope and sources without reading sensitive details.
- **cost:** Approximately $0.03–$0.20 per rehearsal, dominated by authenticated browser extraction and synthesis; cache source snapshots and use deterministic code for arithmetic to keep repeated comparisons inexpensive.
- **security:** This could expose private mail, finances, health, or work information. Require an explicit source scope per rehearsal, keep raw evidence local where possible, attach citations and timestamps, encrypt any relay packet, expire snapshots, and never send, purchase, submit, or edit anything. Forecasts must be labeled as forecasts; conflicting data must be shown rather than silently resolved.
- **missing:** A scenario/rehearsal job type that separates observations, assumptions, options, forecasts, and sensitivity analysis; A cross-surface source-scope and consent envelope carried from the pendant through relay, Mac, and browser; A provenance graph linking every comparison claim to a local file, calendar item, or browser citation; A deterministic what-if executor for dates, costs, capacity, and dependencies; A review UI and compact spoken summary that lets the owner change one assumption and rerun the comparison


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `hardware` — Add a real two-position microphone privacy switch that electrically gates the mic bias/input, plus a small vibration motor and driver on the currently free GPIO/I2C expansion. Expose the switch state to firmware and relay it as an unforgeable input; use vibration patterns for private-mode acknowledgement and completion, while retaining the LED for visible state.
- **owner gets:** The owner can trust that a private conversation is not being captured and can receive a result discreetly in public. A physical mute position remains understandable and effective even if firmware, the relay, or the model misbehaves; haptics make the pendant usable without speaking or looking at it.
- effort: Prototype a mic power/input gate, debounce and report the switch, add a low-power ERM/LRA driver, revise enclosure and battery test, then thread immutable mute state through the audio and job protocols. Validate that gating does not corrupt the shared full-duplex I2S path.  ·  risk: A poorly isolated gate could inject noise or leave the mic partially live; a stuck switch could make the owner think they are private when they are not. Show an unmistakable LED state, run an electrical self-test at boot, and fail closed (no capture) when the state is unknown. Haptic vibration adds mechanical noise into the mic, so never vibrate during capture and characterize coupling.
- cost: About $2–$8 in prototype components and modest assembly cost; roughly 1–10 mA while vibrating and effectively zero for the passive switch. No meaningful API cost.  ·  latency: Hardware mute is immediate; firmware acknowledgement should be under 100 ms. Haptic events add no model latency and can reduce spoken confirmations.
- security: Strongly positive: physical capture prevention and a trustworthy local signal. The switch state must be included in signed/attested device events and never overridden by server commands. Haptic patterns must not encode sensitive content.
- depends on: An output-channel/privacy policy in the shared job envelope; Firmware support for a local mute state and haptic driver; A Mac private-result surface and relay push/completion event path

## Its own summary

Round 14 produced three new, owner-facing directions: (1) a decision journal that captures consequential spoken decisions, assumptions, evidence, and later outcome checks; (2) a cross-surface “keep this private” mode that routes detailed results to the Mac and only gives a discreet pendant signal; and (3) hardware privacy primitives—a physical mic mute and haptic motor—so privacy is enforceable and usable in public. I verified the prototype has one button/LED, free I2C/SPI, and one shared full-duplex I2S peripheral, so haptics and mute need careful electrical/audio isolation. What I still need is not another model: a policy-carrying job/output envelope, durable event and decision-record persistence, provenance-preserving browser evidence binding, Mac private-result UI, push completion/review scheduling, and a firmware/hardware path for local mute and haptic state. I also need the owner’s preferred privacy timeout and whether decision capture should default to off or confirmation-first.

**Biggest unknown:** Whether the owner wants private mode and decision capture to be explicit every time or allowed as configurable, sensitivity-aware defaults; this determines the pendant interaction and retention model.
