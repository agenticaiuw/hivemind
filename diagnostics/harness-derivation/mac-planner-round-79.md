## Capabilities it proposed

### "“If my browser disconnects, pause any private web task safely; when it reconnects, tell me what is stale and let me resume only the steps I choose.”"
- **useful because:** Today the bridge is offline while five commands remain pending, and reconnecting gives no owner-visible reconciliation. This prevents stale clicks or submissions after a long outage, preserves authenticated work across a sleeping Mac, and makes resume an explicit, comprehensible choice rather than an accidental replay.
- **path:** pendant → relay → mac-planner → browser-extension → dashboard
- **model tier:** Use the cheaper background model for queue reconciliation, age/fingerprint comparison, and summaries; use realtime only for the brief spoken status and the owner's resume/cancel utterance.
- **latency:** On heartbeat restoration, quarantine and classify pending work in under 2 seconds; spoken status in under 1 second after the owner asks. Resume starts only after a fresh tab/session witness and should report each step within 2 seconds.
- **cost:** Very low: mostly D1/local state and heartbeat traffic; roughly <$0.001 per reconnect reconciliation, with model cost only when summarization or conflict explanation is needed.
- **security:** Never send page contents or session cookies to the relay; keep task payloads and DOM fingerprints on the Mac/browser. Relay stores only opaque task and command IDs, age, status, and expiry. A stale or changed tab must stop before any mutation. Cancellation and deletion should be available from the pendant and dashboard.
- **missing:** A durable per-task quarantine ledger that records bridge epoch, command age, tab/session affinity, and whether a command is read, reversible, or irreversible; A reconnect handshake that atomically marks commands stale, cancels the five currently pending commands, and issues a fresh witness before resume; Typed conflict results (tab gone, URL changed, DOM fingerprint changed, session expired) and a pendant/dashboard review card with per-step resume selection; A local retention/expiry sweeper and an explicit owner-visible audit trail for quarantined commands

### "“When I leave my Mac halfway through something, give me a private handoff I can resume from the pendant later—what I was doing, what is unfinished, and the exact next safe step.”"
- **useful because:** The owner currently loses the thread when moving between desk, pendant, and browser. Existing jobs and receipts describe actions after the fact, but they do not capture the owner's active attention, unfinished intent, or a compact resume point across surfaces. This would make the wearable a continuity device rather than merely a remote control, without requiring the owner to remember which app or tab was involved.
- **path:** mac-planner → mac-vision → browser-extension → relay → pendant → dashboard
- **model tier:** Use a cheaper background model to compress local app/tab state and completed receipts into a handoff capsule. Use realtime only when the owner asks the pendant to resume or clarify the next step.
- **latency:** Capture a capsule within 3 seconds of an explicit 'pause' gesture or detected Mac departure; pendant retrieval under 1 second for metadata and under 3 seconds for spoken summary. Resume must stop at the first uncertain or mutating step.
- **cost:** Low: mostly local serialization and relay metadata, with one small summarization call per handoff. Approximately $0.001–$0.01 per capsule depending on context size; storage and model summarization dominate.
- **security:** The capsule may expose sensitive app names, URLs, draft text, or private-page snippets. Keep full contents encrypted on the Mac; relay stores only an opaque capsule ID, expiry, sensitivity class, and completion state. Require explicit owner invocation to speak sensitive details aloud, redact secrets and tokens, and never replay a browser mutation merely because it was in the handoff.
- **missing:** A local attention-capture contract that accepts explicit pause plus optional departure detection and records foreground app, relevant browser tab/session, draft locations, and the last confirmed safe step; A cross-surface handoff capsule schema with encrypted Mac-local payload, provenance, TTL, sensitivity labels, and a single resumable next-step pointer; Pendant commands for list, summarize, pin, expire, and resume-capsule; resume must return to Mac/browser only after fresh reachability and tab/session witnesses; A dashboard view that lets the owner edit or delete a capsule and see exactly which context will be restored


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `mac-harness` — Add an Accessibility identity-integrity watchdog to the Mac agent. At startup and before any ui_* action, compare the bundle identity actually posting events (com.aipendant.agent), the authorized Accessibility identity, and the executable path; run a harmless zero-delta probe, persist the result as an input-reachability witness, and make receipts say 'not executed' when the probe fails. If mismatched, open the exact System Settings Privacy & Security > Accessibility pane and show the owner the one binary that must be enabled; after the owner changes it, re-probe and publish a new witness to the relay. Send only status/path hashes to the relay, never screen contents. This is observability and truthful execution, not an approval gate: FULL_CONTROL_MODE remains unchanged.
- **owner gets:** Right now ui_click/type_text can report success while doing nothing: live observation says Accessibility is untrusted because permission belongs to a different binary, and screen recording is also off. The owner would stop getting false claims that work was completed and could repair the Mac in one guided step.
- effort: Small-to-medium: local-agent permission module and receipt fields, a System Settings deep link, startup/periodic probe, and relay/dashboard status card; about 1–2 engineering days plus a few manual permission tests.  ·  risk: The probe must be side-effect-free and must not steal focus. A bad identity comparison could unnecessarily report unavailable; recover by retaining raw diagnostics locally and allowing a manual re-probe. Opening Settings is reversible and does not change permissions automatically.
- cost: Negligible API cost; a few local probes per hour and small status events. No hardware cost.  ·  latency: Adds roughly 50–200 ms before UI actions when a cached healthy witness exists; a failed probe is reported immediately instead of wasting a 45-second UI action timeout.
- security: Improves security posture by preventing untrusted UI receipts and exposing only bundle identifiers/path hashes upstream. It does not add a confirmation gate or reduce the owner's existing maximum-control policy.
- depends on: mac_readonly_inspect implementation or an equivalent local permission inspection path; A stable receipt schema for inputReachability witness IDs; Owner granting Accessibility to the exact AI Pendant Agent binary when they want UI automation

### `context` — Create a privacy-preserving cross-surface handoff capsule protocol. The Mac agent locally watches only foreground-app transitions, accessibility reachability, active browser session IDs, and action receipts—not continuous screen or microphone content. On an explicit pendant 'pause here' command, it snapshots a typed checkpoint: intent label, unfinished step, safe next action, provenance, sensitivity, expiry, and a cryptographic hash of the local encrypted payload. The relay routes the opaque capsule to the pendant and dashboard; on 'resume', Mac and browser must each issue fresh reachability/session witnesses before restoring context. Capsules become immutable after capture, support owner edits as a new version, and expire automatically.
- **owner gets:** They can leave a task at the desk and pick it up from the pendant without losing the exact place or exposing an always-on recording of their screen. Today receipts show what happened, not what the owner meant to continue, so the next action has to be reconstructed manually.
- effort: Medium: define the capsule schema and versioning, add local encrypted storage and TTL cleanup, add relay routing and pendant/dashboard list/summarize/resume messages, and connect fresh Mac/browser witnesses to resume. Roughly 3–5 engineering days.  ·  risk: Foreground-app metadata can still be sensitive, and an incorrect inferred next step could cause confusion. Make explicit pause the default trigger, show the capsule summary before restore, keep the next step advisory unless explicitly resumed, and recover by deleting or reverting to an earlier capsule version.
- cost: Negligible infrastructure cost; compact encrypted local records and small opaque relay records. One inexpensive summarization call only when the owner requests a spoken summary.  ·  latency: Explicit capture under 3 seconds; list/metadata under 1 second; full summary 2–3 seconds. No continuous model or network inference loop.
- security: Improves privacy by excluding screen capture, microphone audio, cookies, and page bodies from the relay. Encrypt the local payload with the Mac keychain, use opaque IDs upstream, redact draft text by default, and enforce expiry/deletion.
- depends on: A durable local encrypted capsule store and schema; Pendant message intents for pause/list/summarize/resume/delete; Fresh witness fields from Mac input reachability and browser session heartbeat; A dashboard review surface
