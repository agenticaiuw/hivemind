## Capabilities it proposed

### "“Start this task now, and if the connection or my Mac drops, safely pick it up later—tell me exactly where it stopped and ask me only when my approval is needed.”"
- **useful because:** Today a long task can outlive a voice session but cannot carry a bounded authority decision across relay, Mac, and authenticated browser. A continuation lease lets the owner leave without losing work while preventing stale permissions from silently submitting or exposing private data.
- **path:** pendant: captures the goal and gives local start/pause/resume gestures plus a short completion/failure voice receipt → relay: stores the signed intent capsule, expiry, privacy class, evidence checkpoints, and wake-up notifications while the owner and Mac are offline → mac-planner: decomposes the goal into resumable semantic steps, records preconditions and evidence, and reconciles after restart rather than replaying clicks → browser-extension: executes only owner-private steps in the bound tab/session, returns URL/title/locator/before-after evidence, and pauses at irreversible checkpoints → dashboard: shows lease scope, expiry, current step, evidence, and approve/resume/revoke controls
- **model tier:** Background planner for decomposition/reconciliation; cheap extraction model for evidence normalization; realtime only for the pendant's live confirmation and spoken receipt.
- **latency:** Start acknowledgement under 2 seconds on the pendant. Resume after reconnect within 10 seconds for state discovery; private-page reattachment may take up to 30 seconds. Approval prompts must be immediate and never expire silently.
- **cost:** Roughly $0.01–$0.08 per resumed task depending on evidence volume; browser and Mac calls dominate, not the planner. Keep only hashes/snippets in the lease and fetch full page text on demand to control context cost.
- **security:** The capsule must contain least-privilege allowed actions, target domains/apps, expiry, and sensitivity. Never carry browser cookies or full private page contents to relay. Require fresh pendant confirmation for send/submit/purchase/delete, revoke on lease expiry, and redact evidence in spoken receipts. A lost pendant must not authorize continuation.
- **missing:** A shared continuation-lease schema and signature/nonce verification across relay, Mac planner, and browser bridge; Semantic checkpoint adapters for Mac actions and browser steps, with restart reconciliation instead of replay; Pendant resume/pause gesture and local lease-status indication; Relay durable wake/resume worker and event stream; Dashboard lease/revoke/approval UI; Authenticated browser result envelopes with locator and content hash

### "“What was I in the middle of, and get me back to the exact place?”"
- **useful because:** The owner often abandons a thought when a meeting, commute, or interruption arrives. Today the Mac, private browser, relay history, and pendant conversation do not form a single recoverable thread; the owner must search tabs and memory manually. This would reconstruct the unfinished intent, distinguish completed from merely viewed work, reopen the right private tabs/files, and offer the next safe step without pretending that an observed page means an action was completed.
- **path:** pendant: owner asks naturally and receives a short spoken reconstruction with confidence and one suggested next step → relay: correlates recent voice intents, job events, Mac checkpoints, and browser evidence into a privacy-scoped activity thread while the Mac may be asleep → mac-planner: inspects local windows/files/jobs and computes the unfinished-work hypothesis, reopening only the selected workspace → browser-extension: reports authenticated tab identity, scroll/selection/form state, and evidence of unsaved or unsubmitted work without sending contents to the relay → dashboard: displays a timeline with evidence, uncertainty, and explicit actions to reopen, discard, or continue
- **model tier:** Cheap background summarizer and deterministic event correlation for the initial reconstruction; a stronger planner only when multiple competing threads need judgment; realtime model only for the pendant exchange.
- **latency:** Spoken answer in 3 seconds for a recent thread; up to 15 seconds for cross-surface reconciliation. Never block on a remote browser if a locally evidenced partial answer can be spoken first.
- **cost:** About $0.005–$0.04 per reconstruction; event correlation and local metadata dominate, with model tokens reserved for ambiguous threads. Do not upload full page bodies or audio by default.
- **security:** Private browser contents, clipboard, microphone audio, and window titles can reveal sensitive work. Keep raw evidence on the Mac/browser, send relay only encrypted references, hashes, and minimal summaries, honor per-domain exclusion rules, and require confirmation before reopening a sensitive tab or restoring unsaved form data. Mark hypotheses as uncertain rather than claiming completion.
- **missing:** A cross-surface activity-thread event schema with provenance and retention limits; Mac window/file/editor-state observer and checkpoint exporter; Browser bridge support for non-destructive tab/form/scroll/selection state capture; Relay correlation store and privacy-aware summarizer; Pendant utterance intent plus dashboard timeline/reopen/discard controls


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `integration` — Introduce a cross-surface Continuation Lease protocol. The relay mints a short-lived, owner-confirmed lease containing a nonce, goal hash, permitted surfaces/domains/apps, allowed effect classes, expiry and approval requirements. Mac and browser workers must attach the lease nonce plus a semantic checkpoint ID and evidence hash to every result; after restart they can only reconcile from the latest checkpoint and must reject stale or replayed action IDs. The pendant can pause/revoke locally, and the relay emits a compact event stream for the dashboard and spoken receipt.
- **owner gets:** A dropped link, sleeping Mac, or closed browser no longer loses a half-finished task or causes it to repeat an action. The owner can leave confidently, return to an exact explanation of what happened, and revoke authority from the pendant without opening the dashboard.
- effort: Medium-high: shared schema/signing library, D1 lease/checkpoint tables, relay resume worker, Mac planner/executor adapters, browser result envelope changes, pendant gesture/status firmware, dashboard state and tests. Start with read-only/reversible tasks before irreversible approvals.  ·  risk: Clock skew, duplicate delivery, stale browser tabs, and malformed checkpoints could pause work or falsely report completion. Use relay-issued monotonic sequence numbers, idempotency keys, explicit UNKNOWN state, lease expiry, and a recovery path that requires fresh owner confirmation rather than guessing. Roll back by treating missing/invalid leases as pause-only and retaining existing job receipts.
- cost: Low relay storage/CPU; perhaps tens of KB per active task plus bounded evidence hashes. Planner calls add roughly $0.01–$0.08 per resumed task; browser/Mac execution remains the dominant cost. No new per-turn realtime spend.  ·  latency: Negligible for live turns (one lease check); reconnect adds ~1–10 seconds for state reconciliation and up to ~30 seconds for authenticated tab reattachment.
- security: Improves security by constraining authority and limiting relay data, but signing-key protection and nonce handling become critical. Keep private page bodies on Mac/browser, encrypt sensitive checkpoint metadata at rest, and require pendant confirmation for irreversible effects.
- depends on: Durable browser job runner with job IDs/retries and result stream; Mac semantic preconditions/checkpoint support (current receipts/undo are not gates); Relay durable job/wake runner; Pendant local pause/revoke/resume gesture; Typed task-relevant context projection to avoid resending private context

### `memory` — Add a provenance-first Activity Thread ledger shared by pendant, relay, Mac, and browser. Each event records thread candidate, source surface, local timestamp/monotonic sequence, evidence kind (voice intent, window/file focus, tab/form state, job result), sensitivity class, and expiry. A correlator periodically emits hypotheses with confidence and explicit gaps; it never upgrades 'viewed' or 'typed' into 'sent' or 'done'. Raw private payloads stay at their source, while the relay stores only encrypted references and minimal redacted summaries. The Mac and browser can consume a selected thread to restore a workspace non-destructively.
- **owner gets:** After an interruption, the owner can recover the exact unfinished thought instead of hunting through tabs and files or accidentally repeating a completed action. The system can say what it knows, what it only suspects, and put the owner back at the right place safely.
- effort: High: event contracts and retention policy, Mac observer/checkpoint exporter, browser bridge state capture, relay correlation and encrypted reference store, dashboard timeline, pendant intent routing, and end-to-end privacy tests.  ·  risk: Over-correlation could join unrelated sensitive work, and restoring stale editor or form state could lose changes. Mitigate with per-app/domain opt-in, short TTLs, cryptographic event IDs, confidence thresholds, read-only preview first, and explicit confirmation before reopening or restoring unsaved state. If any source is unavailable, show a partial timeline rather than infer.
- cost: Small durable metadata footprint; model cost roughly $0.005–$0.04 per reconstruction, dominated by ambiguous summarization. Raw content remains local, reducing relay storage and token cost.  ·  latency: Recent local recovery can be under 3 seconds; cross-surface correlation up to 15 seconds. Event append is asynchronous and should not affect voice latency.
- security: Adds a sensitive activity ledger, so encryption, domain/app exclusion, local deletion, and owner-visible retention controls are mandatory. Relay must not receive raw private page text, clipboard contents, or microphone recordings by default.
- depends on: Typed context projection with provenance and TTL; Browser bridge state/evidence envelope; Mac observer/checkpoint APIs; Relay durable encrypted metadata store; Dashboard timeline and pendant intent routing
