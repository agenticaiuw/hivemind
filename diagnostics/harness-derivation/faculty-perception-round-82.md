## Capabilities it proposed

### "When you say something is done, prove it across the Mac, browser, relay, and pendant—or tell me exactly which link is unverified and why."
- **useful because:** Today a UI action can return a success-shaped receipt while doing nothing, the browser can be offline with commands queued, and old pendant telemetry can look current even when no pendant is registered. This gives the owner a trustworthy answer instead of a plausible status message: observed on screen, acknowledged by the target, delivered to the pendant, or stale/unverified.
- **path:** pendant: emits delivery/playback acknowledgements when connected and preserves an offline event sequence → relay: correlates one operation id across Mac, browser, and pendant events, applies freshness windows, and exposes contradictions → mac-planner: gathers job/action receipts and the live /observe permission/reachability snapshot → browser-extension: returns command acceptance plus tab/session result and heartbeat state → dashboard: presents an evidence chain and blocks the word 'done' when only intent or a stale receipt exists
- **model tier:** deterministic evidence correlator first; background gpt-4.1-mini only to phrase a concise explanation of a contradiction; realtime only when the owner asks live
- **latency:** Under 300 ms for local status classification; under 2 s when waiting for a remote/browser/pendant acknowledgement. Never wait indefinitely—return partial evidence with an expiry.
- **cost:** Usually $0 because correlation is deterministic; roughly $0.001–$0.01 only when a background model turns multiple contradictory records into prose. Storage is small append-only event metadata; audio is never copied into the evidence record.
- **security:** Do not expose page contents or secret values in the chain; retain hashes, URLs/tab IDs, timestamps, action type, and short redacted snippets. Browser and pendant acknowledgements must be authenticated and operation-scoped to prevent a late event from falsely completing a new job. Destructive actions still require the existing confirmation gate.
- **missing:** A durable cross-surface operation/event ID propagated into every Mac, browser, relay, and pendant event; Authenticated pendant delivery/playback acknowledgements and a live device-registry read in the correlation path; A typed outcome schema (intent, accepted, executed, observed, delivered, stale, contradicted, unverified) and freshness policy; A dashboard/API endpoint that returns the evidence chain rather than only the last receipt

### "Tell me when a handoff silently fails—when a command was accepted but never reached the browser, Mac, relay, or pendant—without making me poll or wait for a misleading success message."
- **useful because:** The current system can show a completed-looking local step while the next surface is offline or unreachable. The owner needs detection of missing evidence, not another receipt: a command that never reaches its target should become an actionable alert with the exact broken link and a safe recovery suggestion.
- **path:** pendant: displays or speaks a compact 'handoff failed' alert when it reconnects, preserving the original operation ID and sequence → relay: maintains expected handoff edges and deadlines, detects missing acknowledgements and late/out-of-order events, and queues alerts while the pendant is offline → mac-planner: declares the intended target and expected acknowledgement when starting a job, without claiming completion → browser-extension: emits signed receipt-of-acceptance and receipt-of-effect events tied to the operation ID → mac-vision: if Accessibility is available, emits an independent observation of the resulting screen state; otherwise explicitly reports that observation is unavailable → dashboard: shows a live handoff graph with the first missing edge, deadline, retry status, and owner-approved recovery option
- **model tier:** deterministic event/deadline monitor; use gpt-4.1-mini only to summarize a multi-link failure; reserve realtime for speaking an urgent alert to a connected pendant
- **latency:** Register an expectation under 100 ms; detect a missed local handoff at its deadline (typically 2–10 s), then notify within 1 s. Offline pendant alerts wait safely for reconnection rather than pretending delivery.
- **cost:** Near-zero for the event monitor and durable metadata; approximately $0.001–$0.01 only for an unusually complex natural-language incident summary. No page contents or audio need be retained.
- **security:** Operation IDs and acknowledgements must be authenticated and scoped to prevent forged completion or denial events. Alerts must redact private page contents and secrets. Retries must be idempotent, and any recovery that sends, deletes, purchases, or submits still requires confirmation.
- **missing:** A declared handoff graph and per-edge deadline/expected-event schema; Durable, authenticated acceptance and effect acknowledgements from browser, Mac, relay, and pendant; A reconnect-safe alert queue with deduplication and escalation rules; An observation-vs-intent distinction for GUI actions when Accessibility and Screen Recording are unavailable


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `integration` — Add a read-only Reality Boundary service that snapshots /ops/status, /observe, /browser/status, live relay device registry, and pipeline/job receipts at operation start and completion. It stamps each source with observedAt, source generation, and expiry; joins records by operationId; rejects historical pendant events as current delivery; and emits a typed boundary verdict without executing or blocking actions. Expose GET /reality/:operationId and include the verdict in receipts and spoken responses.
- **owner gets:** The owner will stop hearing 'done' when the browser was offline, the pendant was absent, or Mac UI input was rejected. They get a short honest answer such as 'planned and uploaded, but not delivered' plus the exact missing link.
- effort: Medium: event schema and correlator, relay registry adapter, Mac-agent read-only endpoint, and receipt/dashboard rendering. No Accessibility grant is needed to report the limitation honestly.  ·  risk: Clock skew, late acknowledgements, or reused IDs could misclassify events. Mitigate with server-issued operation IDs, monotonic per-device sequence numbers, bounded freshness windows, and an explicit 'contradicted' state; never silently upgrade to done.
- cost: Negligible compute/storage; deterministic. Optional background wording costs about $0.001–$0.01 per complicated contradiction. No audio payload retention.  ·  latency: ~100–300 ms for local snapshots; remote acknowledgements can be polled up to a fixed 2 s then reported partial.
- security: Store only redacted metadata, hashes, tab IDs, and permission states. Authenticate device acknowledgements and scope them to operation IDs; do not persist private page text or secret values.
- depends on: Durable operationId propagation through Mac/browser/relay events; Live relay device-registry read (currently no pendant is registered); Typed freshness/acknowledgement semantics for pendant and browser events

### `relay` — Add a partition-reconciliation ledger with a per-operation causal chain and exactly-once delivery state. During an outage it records intent, accepted, attempted, and unknown separately; on reconnect it exchanges device sequence ranges, detects duplicates and gaps, and produces one deterministic reconciliation outcome (applied once, safely replayable, or needs owner review) rather than replaying queued commands blindly.
- **owner gets:** When connectivity returns, the owner will not accidentally send the same message or form twice, nor wonder whether an offline pendant command happened. They receive one concise post-outage account of what definitely happened, what did not, and what requires review.
- effort: High: relay D1 schema and reconciliation protocol, client sequence persistence in browser/Mac/pendant firmware, idempotency handling in job execution, and a review view for ambiguous effects.  ·  risk: A device can lose its local counter or reconnect with corrupted state; fail closed to 'needs review' rather than replaying. Some external systems cannot provide idempotency, so those operations must remain pending for confirmation.
- cost: Low ongoing compute; modest D1 metadata growth. No model call for normal reconciliation; optional background summarization costs under $0.01 per incident.  ·  latency: No added latency on the normal path beyond writing a compact ledger record; reconnect reconciliation typically under 2 seconds, with unresolved cases surfaced rather than blocking unrelated work.
- security: Bind counters and operation IDs to authenticated device keys; encrypt sensitive intent metadata; retain hashes and redacted action descriptors rather than form contents or credentials.
- depends on: Authenticated device identity and persistent monotonic sequence numbers; A shared operation/event schema across relay, Mac, browser, and pendant; Idempotency keys accepted by job and browser action runners; Owner-review state for externally observable operations that cannot be proven exactly once
