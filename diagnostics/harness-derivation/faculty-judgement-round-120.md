## Capabilities it proposed

### "Before you brief me or act, tell me what is actually true across my pendant, Mac, browser, and relay—flag contradictions and stale status instead of confidently repeating them, and give me one safe next step to repair each problem."
- **useful because:** Today the system can simultaneously report a completed/healthy workflow, an offline browser with queued commands, a stale pipeline stuck in processing, and a briefing claiming Calendar/Mail grants are missing even though the live permission snapshot says automation is granted. The owner needs a trustworthy decision, not a fluent synthesis of conflicting records.
- **path:** faculty-perception → relay-realtime → mac-planner → browser-extension → mac-terminal → faculty-judgement → faculty-action → unified
- **model tier:** Background/cheap model performs claim extraction, timestamp comparison, and contradiction clustering; realtime is used only to speak the short, owner-facing verdict when requested. Escalate to the expensive tier only when contradictions affect an irreversible action.
- **latency:** Under 2 seconds from cached snapshots; under 10 seconds when refreshing Mac/browser/relay evidence. Never block an urgent spoken reply on a nonessential source.
- **cost:** Usually <$0.01 per reconciliation using a small model over compact typed claims; occasional realtime speech dominates interactive cost. Storage is a few KB per snapshot/claim, with short retention for sensitive browser facts.
- **security:** Authenticated page contents, mail/calendar metadata, and device telemetry must stay in the existing private surfaces; send only hashes/typed claims to the reconciliation layer where possible. Never expose secrets or infer that a queued command ran. Require confirmation before repair actions, browser submissions, mail, deletion, or replaying pending commands.
- **missing:** A durable, provenance-aware claim ledger with source timestamps, TTLs, and status values (current, stale, contradicted, unreachable).; A reconciliation pass that compares /ops/status permissions against generated briefings and compares pipeline state against relay/device acknowledgements.; A spoken-safe formatter that suppresses contradicted claims and emits evidence plus a reversible repair proposal.; A repair workflow that rechecks preconditions immediately before action and records the resulting receipt.

### "When something I asked for goes wrong or arrives late, explain the incident in plain language—what I said, which surface received it, where it stalled, whether anything actually changed, and the safest retry—without repeating an action automatically."
- **useful because:** A failed spoken request currently leaves the owner guessing whether the pendant heard it, the relay accepted it, the Mac acted, or the browser was offline. A causal timeline turns opaque failures and late deliveries into a recoverable decision, especially when the owner is away from the Mac.
- **path:** pendant → relay-realtime → mac-planner → mac-terminal → browser-extension → faculty-perception → faculty-judgement → faculty-action → unified
- **model tier:** Use a cheap background model to correlate event IDs, timestamps, receipts, and acknowledgements; use realtime only to deliver a one-sentence incident summary and ask for confirmation when retry could duplicate an action.
- **latency:** Return an initial bounded status in 1 second from stored events; complete timeline correlation within 5 seconds. If a source is unreachable, explicitly say so rather than waiting indefinitely.
- **cost:** <$0.01 per incident for compact event correlation; no model call for simple receipt lookup. Storage is small append-only event metadata, with payload redaction and retention limits.
- **security:** Do not include private page text, mail bodies, or secret command arguments in shared incident reports. Redact sensitive fields, preserve source-local evidence, and require confirmation for retries that might send, purchase, delete, or replay a browser command.
- **missing:** A stable correlation ID propagated from pendant utterance through relay job, Mac job, browser command, pipeline events, audio delivery, and receipt.; A causal-state vocabulary distinguishing accepted, queued, started, blocked, delivered, played, expired, cancelled, and unknown.; A duplicate-risk checker and owner-facing retry preview.

### "Before I commit to something, let me rehearse the consequences: check my calendar, current commitments, private messages, travel or account details, and active browser work; show what this choice would collide with, what it would make harder, and a few safe alternatives—without changing anything."
- **useful because:** The owner can ask the system to execute isolated tasks, but cannot yet ask it to reason about a life decision across all of the places where consequences are distributed. A rehearsal would prevent accidental overbooking, missed obligations, contradictory replies, and commitments made without seeing the relevant private context. It is advice and simulation, not autonomous action.
- **path:** pendant → relay-realtime → mac-planner → browser-extension → mac-terminal → faculty-perception → faculty-judgement → faculty-action → unified
- **model tier:** Use a cheaper background reasoning model to gather and normalize facts and generate multiple scenarios; use the realtime tier only for the short spoken result and follow-up questions. Escalate to the expensive tier only when evidence conflicts or the decision has high stakes.
- **latency:** A quick rehearsal in 5 seconds from cached context; a thorough one in 20 seconds with fresh private-page checks. The pendant should give an immediate acknowledgement while the deeper analysis runs.
- **cost:** Roughly $0.01–$0.05 per rehearsal, dominated by authenticated browser reads and synthesis rather than speech. Cache stable obligations and only refresh volatile sources.
- **security:** Keep mail, calendar, account, and page contents on their owning surfaces; pass typed facts and redacted excerpts to the judge where possible. Clearly label inference versus evidence. No messages, bookings, edits, or submissions occur in rehearsal mode; any conversion to action requires a new confirmation and a fresh precondition check.
- **missing:** A read-only scenario/rehearsal contract that takes a proposed commitment plus constraints and returns impacts, uncertainties, and alternatives.; A cross-surface obligation model that links calendar events, reminders, messages, browser transactions, travel details, and active work without copying sensitive payloads unnecessarily.; A temporal consequence engine for detecting collisions, deadlines, dependencies, and opportunity costs.; An explicit boundary in the action layer preventing rehearsal outputs from becoming queued mutations, plus provenance for every cited consequence.


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `context` — Add a cross-surface Truth Ledger and gate every spoken brief/action plan on it. Each observation becomes a typed claim {subject,predicate,value,source,observedAt,expiresAt,confidence,sensitivity}; reconciliation marks claims current, stale, contradicted, or unreachable. For contradictions, retain both evidence records, prefer direct live probes over cached/generated artifacts, and require a fresh probe before any dependent action. The formatter must say 'I have conflicting reports' rather than selecting a plausible value. Add a repair transaction that refreshes the relevant source, re-evaluates preconditions, and records a receipt without replaying queued browser commands.
- **owner gets:** The pendant would stop telling you that Mail needs a grant when the live Mac snapshot says it has one, stop calling a dead browser online, and stop treating a half-open pipeline as completed. You get a short, honest answer and a safe recovery path instead of discovering later that the system acted on stale state.
- effort: Medium: typed claim schema, a small D1/local JSON ledger, reconciliation rules, and adapters for existing status/briefing/pipeline/browser routes; then add a pre-speech and pre-action hook.  ·  risk: Incorrect TTLs could make the assistant over-cautious or hide a real update. Recover by preserving raw evidence, allowing an explicit 'refresh and decide' command, and failing closed only for irreversible actions. Avoid deleting existing records.
- cost: Negligible storage and small-model classification cost; compact claims reduce repeated context tokens. Live refreshes add API calls only when a claim is stale or action-critical.  ·  latency: No noticeable delay from cached reconciliation; 1–10 seconds for source refresh, with immediate provisional wording if the owner is waiting.
- security: Improves security by preventing stale/private browser evidence from being presented as current and by keeping page contents on their owning surface. Claim sensitivity labels must prevent secrets from entering shared prompts or audio.
- depends on: A durable cross-surface event/receipt identity shared by relay, Mac, browser, and pendant; A freshness policy for each source and explicit precedence rules; Existing /ops/status, /briefing/latest, /browser/status, /pipeline, and job receipt adapters

### `interaction` — Introduce a visibly separate `REHEARSE` conversation state spanning pendant, relay, Mac, and browser. In this state, all retrieved facts are assembled into an immutable scenario snapshot; the judge may branch alternatives and calculate consequences, but the action router is structurally unable to enqueue mutations. The owner can say `make this real` only after reviewing a compact delta that lists every proposed change, affected surface, evidence age, uncertainty, and duplicate risk; that phrase creates a new action plan rather than upgrading the rehearsal in place.
- **owner gets:** They can explore consequential choices while walking or away from the Mac without fear that asking 'what if?' will send a message, alter a calendar, replay a browser command, or spend a scarce approval. The owner gets understandable alternatives first and deliberate execution second.
- effort: Medium-to-large: add a scenario snapshot and branch format to the relay protocol, propagate mode through planner and browser requests, enforce a deny-by-construction mutation guard, then add a review-to-action transition with fresh preconditions.  ·  risk: A stale snapshot could produce an attractive but wrong rehearsal. Show observation times, mark unknowns, refresh volatile facts before conversion to action, and expire snapshots quickly. If mode metadata is lost, fail closed as read-only.
- cost: Small persistent metadata and one additional synthesis call when branching alternatives; no mutation or browser-action cost during rehearsal. Realtime speech remains limited to acknowledgement and summary.  ·  latency: Immediate local acknowledgement; 5–20 seconds for scenario assembly depending on private-source refreshes. Conversion adds a final precondition check.
- security: Strongly reduces accidental side effects and prevents scenario text from being mistaken for executable intent. Sensitive evidence remains source-local and only redacted claims enter the scenario snapshot.
- depends on: A cross-surface typed fact/obligation projection; Stable provenance and freshness metadata; A mutation-deny guard enforced below the planner, not merely in prompts; Existing job, browser, pipeline, and receipt identifiers
