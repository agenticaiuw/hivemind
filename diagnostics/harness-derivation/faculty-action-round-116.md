## Capabilities it proposed

### "“Do this everywhere it needs to happen, and tell me one honest result: completed, waiting for me, or unable — with proof of what changed.”"
- **useful because:** Today a plan can span the relay, Mac AppleScript/shell, and authenticated Safari, yet each surface reports its own success and stale pipeline runs can remain processing. This gives the owner one cross-surface execution contract: faculty-judgement supplies a typed plan, faculty-action leases and executes each step on the only reachable hand, faculty-perception verifies the postcondition from an independent read, and the pendant receives a concise result. UI actions that cannot reach the screen are reported unable rather than receiving a false success receipt.
- **path:** faculty-judgement → faculty-action → faculty-perception → relay-realtime → mac-planner → mac-terminal → browser-extension → unified → pendant
- **model tier:** Use the cheap background/local planner for decomposition and reconciliation; reserve realtime for the owner's live request and the final one-sentence spoken outcome. Do not spend the realtime tier on polling or verification.
- **latency:** Reversible one-surface actions: under 3 seconds. Multi-surface work: stream step states immediately, verify each completed step within 5 seconds, and finish or explicitly block within 30 seconds; long work becomes a durable job with relay_job_status.
- **cost:** Roughly one local/background model call per ambiguous plan plus one short realtime response; most deterministic steps and checks are zero model cost. Dominant cost is private-page extraction or GUI vision, which is skipped when AppleScript/browser DOM reads suffice.
- **security:** Authenticated tab contents and Mac state leave the device only through the existing bearer-protected relay paths; redact secrets from receipts and never persist page bodies by default. Require owner confirmation immediately before send/delete/purchase or any irreversible browser step. A lease expiry, duplicate request ID, failed postcondition, missing Accessibility, offline bridge, or stale tab must end as blocked/unknown—not success.
- **missing:** A shared typed action contract linking planId, stepId, idempotency key, target surface, precondition, postcondition, approval class, lease expiry, and evidence reference.; An independent postcondition verifier that can read state without trusting the executor's success receipt, including browser tab/session affinity and AppleScript-readable Mac state.; A cross-surface receipt reducer that collapses step outcomes into completed/waiting-for-owner/unable and marks stale processing jobs.; Physical pendant pairing/telemetry so the final receipt and approval can be delivered and acknowledged on-device.

### "“Pause this private browser task and let me resume it from the pendant when I’m away from my Mac.”"
- **useful because:** Today a logged-in browser task is tied to the Mac and its live tab; leaving the desk loses the conversational handoff or tempts unsafe re-authentication. The owner should be able to park the exact tab, draft state, and next safe step, then resume from the worn device using a short-lived, device-bound confirmation—without transmitting the page contents into the voice context. This is a new physical handoff, not a page watch, morning brief, durable job runner, or generic approval queue.
- **path:** browser-extension → mac-planner → mac-terminal → relay-realtime → pendant → faculty-judgement → faculty-action → faculty-perception
- **model tier:** Use no expensive model for parking, pairing, or state transfer. Use a cheap background/local model only to summarize the next step and redact it; use realtime only when the owner asks to resume or needs the short spoken prompt.
- **latency:** Parking under 2 seconds; resume prompt under 1 second after the pendant button; reattach and independently verify the tab within 5 seconds. Expire an unattended handoff after 10 minutes or the configured owner limit.
- **cost:** Near-zero model cost for deterministic session transfer and cryptographic checks; one small local-model call only for a redacted task label. Storage is a compact encrypted handoff record, not page content.
- **security:** Never copy authenticated page bodies, cookies, or credentials to the pendant or relay. Bind a single-use resume capability to the paired pendant, Mac bridge, browser session, tab identity, and owner confirmation; invalidate it on tab navigation, logout, session change, or expiry. Irreversible submission still requires a fresh confirmation and independent before/after verification. If pairing or verification fails, report unable and leave the browser untouched.
- **missing:** A paired, connected pendant with a hardware button/LED protocol and secure device identity; no pendant is present in the live device table today.; An encrypted, single-use handoff token service in the relay and a Mac-side session lease that can reattach the exact Safari tab without exposing its contents.; Browser Bridge support for freezing a tab's semantic checkpoint (URL, tab/session ID, redacted next-step label, and form-diff hash) and refusing resume after unexpected navigation.; A pendant-side confirmation/expiry state machine and a faculty-action verifier that proves the resumed tab and intended precondition are still the same before any mutation.


## Changes it proposed to its own stack

### `integration` — Add a single Action Ledger and reconciler between judgement, action, and perception. On /plan, persist a typed DAG of steps with idempotency keys, preconditions, postconditions, approval class, and target surface. Every executor (Mac action, browser command, relay job, or pendant delivery) must append append-only started/blocked/completed evidence. A separate verifier reads the resulting Mac/browser/relay state and emits verified, disproven, or unverifiable. The reducer exposes one terminal outcome and automatically marks jobs with no heartbeat past their lease as interrupted rather than processing forever; undo links to the exact receipt evidence.
- **owner gets:** The owner stops hearing “success” when Accessibility is false, a browser tab changed, or a relay job is merely waiting for approval. They get one honest answer and can resume an interrupted multi-device task without repeating already-completed steps.
- effort: Medium-high: shared schema and D1/local persistence, adapters for existing Mac/browser/pipeline routes, verifier library, dashboard and relay projection, plus failure-injection tests for duplicate delivery and dropped links.  ·  risk: A strict reducer could incorrectly classify a slow job as interrupted or block a legitimate action. Use renewable leases, explicit unknown state, idempotency keys, and manual resume; never auto-retry irreversible steps. Roll back by disabling reconciliation and retaining existing per-surface receipts.
- cost: Negligible storage and no additional model calls for deterministic verification; occasional background/local planner call only for ambiguous postconditions. Small D1/local disk growth from compact evidence, with bounded retention.  ·  latency: Adds roughly 100–500 ms for ledger writes and deterministic verification; avoids expensive vision/replanning when typed evidence proves the result.
- security: Improves least-privilege and auditability, but evidence can contain private URLs or snippets. Encrypt or hash sensitive evidence, redact secrets, enforce per-job access, and require confirmation gates before irreversible adapters.
- depends on: Typed plan/step schema shared by faculty-judgement and faculty-action; Independent verifier (not executor self-report); A pending physical pendant and pairing telemetry for on-device completion/approval; Owner-granted Accessibility only for GUI steps; AppleScript and browser DOM paths remain valid without it

### `new-surface` — Add a paired-pendant Handoff Channel: a hardware-backed, single-use resume capability whose token is minted by the relay, stored only as an encrypted nonce on the pendant, and redeemed by the Mac bridge against an exact browser session/tab checkpoint. The Browser Bridge must expose checkpoint, invalidate, and reattach operations; the pendant shows a two-state LED (parked/resume-ready) and requires a physical button press to redeem. Faculty-action receives only a redacted task label and checkpoint hash, while faculty-perception re-reads the tab before allowing the next mutation.
- **owner gets:** They can walk away from a private browser workflow and pick it up from the pendant without losing their place or leaking private page contents into voice history—and a stale or hijacked tab cannot silently continue.
- effort: High: secure pairing and nonce storage in firmware, relay token/expiry endpoints, Safari bridge checkpoint and reattach protocol, Mac session lease, and end-to-end tests for tab navigation, logout, dropped LTE, duplicate button presses, and replay.  ·  risk: A stolen or mispaired pendant could become a session-resume token. Require explicit pairing, one-device binding, short TTL, one-time redemption, immediate revocation, and a visible parked state. On any mismatch, leave the tab unchanged and require Mac re-authentication. Roll back by disabling the handoff endpoint; existing browser sessions remain unaffected.
- cost: Small relay/D1 writes and negligible bandwidth; no recurring model cost. Firmware adds a few KB of code/state and minimal button/LED power. Encrypted nonce storage must fit the prototype's constrained flash/RAM.  ·  latency: Adds one relay round trip (target under 1 second) and one tab reattachment/verification pass (under 5 seconds); no impact on ordinary browser actions.
- security: Reduces exposure by keeping page data on the Mac, but introduces a high-value capability token. Use hardware-bound keys, nonce replay protection, audit receipts without sensitive content, and mandatory confirmation for irreversible actions.
- depends on: A real paired pendant (the current live device table has none); A secure device-identity/pairing protocol; Safari Browser Bridge checkpoint and exact-tab reattachment APIs; Owner-defined handoff TTL and revocation behavior
