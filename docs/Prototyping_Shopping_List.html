Phase 1 — Prototyping shopping list

Breadboard-friendly parts to prove the system before the custom PCB. You already have the breadboard, jumpers, and LEDs, so those are off the list. All links go straight to the product page.

These are breakout boards (chip + its application circuit + header holes) so you can wire everything on a breadboard with jumper wires — the only soldering is attaching the header strips (easy through-hole). The dev board already includes USB, a LiPo charger, an accelerometer, u.FL antenna jacks, a button, and the eSIM. Prices are rough USD — verify at checkout. Breakouts may use a sibling chip (fine for proving the system; the exact parts go on the PCB later).
