## Actionable issues

1. **Physical microphone mute switch is absent from the device BOM.** `Parts` rows 6–22 include "Touch/squeeze or button input" and "Indicator LEDs," but `docs/hardware/respin-speaker-mute-secure-element.md` specifies a separate physical mic mute switch and warns that its candidate `CUS-12TB` single-pole part does not fully implement the proposed two-pole topology (§3.4, BOM B1). The squeeze/button input is not the privacy mute. Add an explicit candidate mute-switch row, with the selected part and any gasket/boot/board connection cost, or explicitly label physical mute as excluded pending the topology decision. Otherwise the current design scope and per-device cost disagree. The published candidate single-unit switch check was $0.91, but its suitability remains open.

2. **Speaker allowance is below the documented candidate price.** The `Parts` small-speaker line uses $3.00 as an unquoted allowance; the later respin's candidate PUI `AS01308MR-2-R` is recorded at $4.46 at one. For a 36-start small batch, either raise the allowance toward that candidate price or say that the $3 input depends on selecting a different, cheaper speaker that has yet to be sourced. The final prototype still needs fit/acoustic selection, so neither number is a firm quote.

3. **Pilot charging accessories should be available for prototype tests.** The itemized BOM correctly carries *device* charging contacts ($3) and the final `Manufacturing` $18 kit correctly assigns a separate $8 mating cable. The 10 prototype starts also include device contacts but no explicit mating cable. The two $75-per-run bench-consumable lines may cover reusable prototype cable(s); state that in the source note or add a small one-time prototype accessory input. This is a completeness/sequence issue, not a call to buy 10 cables.

4. **Hands-on assembly hours need a visible basis.** The builder assigns 1.5 hours × 10 prototype starts and 0.75 hours × 36 final starts, yielding 15 and 27 hours. The independent manufacturing note allowed 2 hours per final start and broader prototype debug time. The workbook's 582 volunteer hours is explicitly a planning workload, not a commitment, but the user asked for soldering, assembly and testing. Consider increasing or explaining the 45-minute final touch-up/installation/programming/test estimate, especially with a battery, speaker, vents, seal, charge contacts and functional acceptance checks. This changes the in-kind hours, not the cash request, unless paid labor is later introduced.

5. **Use the current DI Lab source URLs in the notes.** The manufacturing research cites `https://making.engr.wisc.edu/3d-printing-di-lab/` and `https://making.engr.wisc.edu/access/m-pass/`, while the builder prints older `di.engr.wisc.edu/makerspace/...` paths. The budget correctly calls $12 per shell set a planning allowance rather than an official campus price; use the same current page paths as the research note so a reviewer can trace the equipment/material and access-rate basis.
