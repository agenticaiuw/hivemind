# Bluetooth candidate pricing and design revision

Checked 2026-09-23. Owner: purchased-components research subagent. Token telemetry unavailable. Machine-readable data: `bluetooth-revision.json`.

The historical invoices cover ESP32 testing and an nRF9160 DK, but the repository's [pendant-v2 design](../../docs/hardware/pendant-v2.md) selects an **nRF5340 BLE peripheral** for the proposed product. Its primary transport is Opus frames over custom GATT to a phone, which relays to the cloud and handles the user's paired earbuds. That document explicitly states the nRF5340 has no Bluetooth Classic/A2DP and that LE Audio capability alone does not solve iOS background routing. Therefore the ESP32 prototype purchase should not be labeled the final Bluetooth component.

Both are **candidate future purchases**, not existing paid invoices or 30 final device line items.

The module's $7.00 quote falling below Nordic's one-unit bare-SoC quote reflects two different supplier listings and terms; it is not proof that the completed circuit costs less to manufacture. The Raytac Marketplace listing has a $25 shipping charge and no visible tier price. Stock and prices should be refreshed before ordering. The v2 enclosure's RF keepout, pin map, 1.8 V mic rail, and real GATT audio throughput remain design validation items. This is a comparison between proposed radio sourcing paths, not a validated final BOM.
