[OSH Park 4-layer public rate](https://docs.oshpark.com/services/four-layer/) provides a size-based benchmark; [JLCPCB thickness capability](https://jlcpcb.com/resources/pcb-thickness) confirms 0.8 mm as a supported 4-layer option. Final price requires routed Gerbers and manufacturer review.”

The transparent public-rate calculation is **32 × 32 mm bounding area** ÷ 645.16 = **1.5872 square inches**. [OSH Park publishes $10 per square inch for a set of three four-layer boards](https://docs.oshpark.com/services/four-layer/). Thirty boards require ten sets, so **1.5872 × $10 × 10 = $158.72**, or **$5.29 per board**. This is a *benchmark*, not a quote for the target board: OSH Park's published stack is **1.6 mm**, while the v2 pendant's mechanical stack requires **0.8 mm**. A circular outline may have a different chargeable area; the 32 mm square is a conservative arithmetic assumption. The proposed **$8 × 30 = $240** is a higher 0.8 mm fabrication allowance, not an advertised manufacturer price. A separate **$45 freight/tax reserve** would make the bare-board landed allowance **$285 / $9.50 per device**, but the main final-table row should use $240 because no shipping destination or purchase route has been quoted. No flat tax rate was invented.

[JLCPCB's thickness guide](https://jlcpcb.com/resources/pcb-thickness) and [four-layer stackup selector](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance) support 0.8 mm four-layer fabrication, but the online teaser for a different board configuration is not usable for this full run. If the final Bluetooth module, outline, finish, impedance rules or via scheme require a larger or more complex board, replace this allowance from a design-specific quote. [PCBWay's assembly form](https://www.pcbway.com/quotesmt.aspx) recommends panelization when a single board side is below 50 mm and states that exact PCBA pricing follows file review. A standalone optional outsource assembly allowance, **$250 setup/stencil/inspection plus $15 × 30 placement/soldering = $700**, is in `pcb-revision.json`; it should be omitted from the user's requested bare-board row and not added where assembly is already costed.
