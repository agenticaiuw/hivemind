# Lower-cost payment hardware check — 2026-09-23

Owner: `additional_components`. Claimed files: this note and `cheap-payment.json`. Research is read-only; no vendor contact, purchasing, DOCX changes or commits. Exact token telemetry is unavailable to this agent, so no usage number is guessed.

The £99 [USC wearable samples pack](https://www.usmartcards.co.uk/sample-and-oem-packs/usc-wearable-technology-samples-pack) is a **pack of evaluation items**, not the cost of a payment chip. There are clearly lower-cost finished consumer payment products, such as the [€29.95 Fidesmo Pay keyring](https://shop.fidesmo.com/products/payment-keyring), but that is a finished and provisioned consumer item, not a loose component or a price for integrating it into this device.

For payment-capable silicon, [Infineon SECORA Pay W](https://www.infineon.com/products/security-smart-card-solutions/secora-security-solutions/secora-pay) is the most fitting passive-wearable candidate: Infineon lists SPA 2.1 insert and USON8-7 chip forms, with pre-certified Visa/Mastercard payment support. Its current public page does **not** quote a single-unit component price. [ST's STPay-Topaz-1 listing](https://www.st.com/en/secure-mcus/stpay-topaz-1.html) has an empty budgetary-price field and reports no distributor availability. [NXP PN80T](https://www.nxp.com/company/about-nxp/newsroom/NW-LEADERSHIP-NFC) integrates NFC and a secure element and is a proven payment-wearable architecture, but I found no defensible authorized-distributor single-unit USD listing. These are quote-only for this cost document. A quote should distinguish the chip/insert, antenna/inlay, payment software, provisioning/setup and per-device fees.

Two inexpensive, publicly priced building blocks must **not** be presented as a payment-wallet substitute: [NXP PN7160 NFC controller](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/nxp-usa-inc/PN7160A1HN-C100E/15650151) is $9.63 at one or $7.9096 at 25+, and [NXP SE050E2 IoT secure element](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/nxp-usa-inc/SE050E2HQ1-Z01Z3Z/16366219) is $4.35 at one or $3.0288 at 25+. Their two-chip parts subtotal would be $13.98 at one or $10.9384 each at 25+, before antenna, PCB, software, certification and provisioning; **it is not a payment-capable BOM**. [NXP expressly says](https://www.nxp.com/products/PN7160) PN7160/PN7161 are not intended or designed for applications requiring EMVCo 3.x analog and digital compliance. The SE050 listing is for general IoT trust functions and does not include an issuer-provisioned payment applet/credential.

Therefore the supportable unit cost entry for a deployable contactless payment chip/insert is **supplier quote required**, not £99 and not an invented low-dollar estimate. If the document wants a visible low price for a payment *demonstration*, the €29.95 Fidesmo keyring can be shown with a clear consumer-product label and issuer/geography caveat; it does not validate custom PCB integration or US card availability.
