# One-piece bare cellular IC check — 2026-09-23

An independent component merchant [lists a bare Qualcomm **MDM9207** BGA IC for **$9.20 for one**](https://tech-electr.com/product/mdm9207-qualcomm-genuine-reliable-electronic-components/), says “Stock Available,” and states MOQ 1. This is a genuine **chip-only price listing**, although merchant authorization, delivered inventory and silicon provenance were not verified. The merchant gives only the family name MDM9207, so the specific stepping is unknown.

[Qualcomm’s primary MDM9207-1 announcement](https://s204.q4cdn.com/645488518/files/doc_news/2015/10/2015-10-26_Qualcomm_Announces_New_Modem_Solutions_Designed_725.pdf) explicitly states that the **MDM9207-1 platform** supports VoLTE. The $9.20 seller listing does **not** establish that the item supplied is this exact -1 variant or that a buyer can obtain compatible modem/IMS firmware. Therefore the defensible table wording is “MDM9207 bare modem IC — $9.20 single-unit seller listing; VoLTE documented for MDM9207-1, exact seller variant and usable firmware unverified.”

An [exact MDM-9207-1-328PSP-TR-00-0 listing at Win Source](https://www.win-source.net/products/detail/qualcomm/mdm-9207-1-328psp-tr-00-0.html) is $8.797 per chip, but its **minimum order is six**, so it is not a one-chip purchase. A [marketplace listing of the exact -1 part](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/Stock-Market-Other-Ics-MDM9207-1_1601517446831.html) shows MOQ 1 and €0.8586, but that unusually low offer has negotiated freight and no independent authenticity or firmware assurance; it should not drive a credible budget. The [OKmarts MDM9607-0VV listing](https://okmarts.com/qualcomm-ic-integrated-circuit-chip-mdm9607-0vv.html) says $11.54 each but **MOQ 28**, despite the per-piece display.

The chip is not a self-contained cellular radio. A [Quectel FCC-filed module bill of materials](https://device.report/m/99b0769112f690fcc6f870d43fd45a3e47f9883d6e32ca86d380e5c86894bf3f.pdf) identifies MDM9207-1 baseband plus separate WTR2965 RF transceiver, PMD9607 power-management IC, LTE PA, RF filters and switches, memory, and clock. A device also needs an antenna, SIM circuitry, PCB/RF layout, accessible firmware, software integration and carrier acceptance. The $9.20 cannot be substituted for the price of a ready-to-use standalone US VoLTE module.

Two alternate bare-IC families have manufacturer VoLTE evidence but no verified exact one-unit retail quote: [UNISOC 8910DM](https://www.unisoc.com/en/product/WA-IoT/8910DM) and [ASR Micro ASR1603](https://testhardware.iot.telekom.com/Document/5313633524454367414). Marketplace price ranges for these chips are too ambiguous to assert a one-chip purchase price.
