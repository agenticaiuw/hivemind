# Candidate student tools

Proposal, not approved product requirements. Source: user's five hardware-team slide screenshots and stated tool-only intent.

Vision: build focused physical tools that help students capture, understand, create and act while retaining control of attention. Screenless is appropriate for some tasks; reading and handwriting benefit from a screen.

Candidates: voice pendant (capture/action); e-paper study notebook (reading/annotation/math); quiet desk dashboard (next commitments); tactile dial/button controller (intentional start/stop/cancel); document capture stand (paper to digital); keyboard writing terminal (long prose); small haptic navigation/timer accessory (brief cues).

Prioritize pendant plus separately scoped e-note project; dashboard/controller are smaller contributor projects. Do not launch every candidate at once. Shared identity, notes/tasks and sync protocol; local essential operation; cloud/phone optional for advanced AI. E-note MVP: import PDF, page navigation, pen/erase/undo, local autosave, export annotated PDF; then explicit selected-content AI and sync. Validate latency, palm rejection and persistence before custom PCB/enclosure. Use matched display/controller/digitizer module for initial integration.

Tool-only constraints: intentional entry, bounded result, clear finish; no feed, engagement prompts or unsolicited AI chatter; contextual AI on request; essential functions offline; user-owned export; visible processing/saved/synced/error states. Metrics: task completion, save/recovery reliability, readability/writing latency, fewer phone pickups, measured learning outcomes—not engagement minutes.
